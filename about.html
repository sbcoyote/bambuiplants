A family of growers with a passion for bamboo.

Bambuiplants is a family-run nursery in Alabama, built around the belief that beautiful, resilient landscapes begin with healthy plants and honest guidance.

Our story

What began as a search for a beautiful, fast-growing privacy screen grew into a lasting appreciation for bamboo’s strength, versatility, and calming presence. We now share that experience with gardeners, landscapers, and DIY growers across the country.

Every rhizome is sourced from our own groves, prepared with care, and sold through our Etsy and eBay stores. We want you to feel confident about what you are planting and how to help it establish.
